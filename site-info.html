Privacy & site information

Privacy

VINAYAK Hair & Skin Studio uses your account name, username, phone number, email when supplied and appointment details to manage your salon bookings. If you sign in with Google, the site stores your verified email and Google account subject ID to recognise your account on later visits; it does not store your Google password. Passwords are stored as hashes. The salon owner can view booking contact details; customer accounts can access only their own account-linked appointments.

If you request WhatsApp or email confirmation, your contact details, appointment information and salon location are sent to the configured messaging provider. WhatsApp is requested only after you select its checkbox; email is requested when you provide an email address. Provider delivery requires the salon to configure its messaging accounts.

Owner-uploaded gallery photos are public while published. Contact the salon owner about your personal information or a photo concerning you.

Google Maps content is loaded from Google, which may receive your IP address and browser information and use its own cookies. Google Maps content and links are subject to Google's Privacy Policy. Live Google rating data is used for display and is not copied into the booking database.

Using this website

Sign in to confirm an available appointment. A booking is reserved only when confirmation and a booking reference appear. Starting prices and consultation-based services should be confirmed with the salon owner. This website does not collect online payments or send SMS. The location appears immediately on the confirmation page and in appointment history; WhatsApp and email delivery depend on configured providers.

For booking changes or account help, contact the salon owner using the contact details on the salon's Google Maps listing.

Use of the embedded Google Maps services is also subject to the Google Maps Additional Terms of Service. Google controls its map content, ratings and review availability.
