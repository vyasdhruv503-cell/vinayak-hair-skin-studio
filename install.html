Install VINAYAK Hair & Skin Studio on your phone. Open bookings, your profile, and our gallery straight from your home screen.

Android

  1. Open the salon’s public website in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install app below when available, or open Chrome’s ⋮ menu and choose Add to Home screen / Install app.
  3. Confirm the installation, then open VINAYAK from your home screen.

iPhone

  1. Open the salon’s public website in Safari.
  2. Tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
  3. If shown, enable Open as Web App, then tap Add.

Live bookings require an internet connection. If your connection drops, check your appointment history when you reconnect.
